Dimitrios Troullinos

Postdoctoral Researcher· Artificial Intelligence Research Institute (IIIA-CSIC) · Spanish National Research Council (CSIC)

Hi! I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the Artificial Intelligence Research Institute, advised by Prof. Juan A. Rodríguez Aguilar and Prof. Ángela Ribeiro, as part of my ongoing MSCA (Cofund) Fellowship for the ALLIES project. My research interests lie in the area of multiagent systems: more specifically in distributed constraint optimization problems and reinforcement learning, with applications in lane-free traffic, and more recently in agricultural robotics for human-robot collaboration. I have a PhD from the Technical University of Crete, advised by Prof. Georgios Chalkiadakis and Prof. Markos Papageorgiou, within the frame of the ERC Advanced Grant Trafficfluid. Previously, I studied Electrical and Computer Engineering (MEng) at the same institute.
